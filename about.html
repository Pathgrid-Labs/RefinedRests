About Refined Rests

Refined Rests (RR) is a community focused bathrooms discovery and review platform. Similar to Yelp, RR allows users to locate nearby public restrooms, share their experiences, and rate bathrooms based on important qualities. Whether you're traveling, exploring a new area, or simply looking for a dependable restroom nearby, RefinedRests helps you make informed decisions through honest, community-based reviews.

Specifications

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Google Maps API
  • Backend: Node.js, Express.js
  • Database: MongoDB
